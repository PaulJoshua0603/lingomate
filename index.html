    Australian Slang & Culture

    Talk like an Aussie

    The expressions you'll actually hear at work, what they mean in Filipino, how to say them, and when to use them.

    Safe at work

    Use anywhere

    "No worries", "reckon", "touch base", "I'm across it". Fine in meetings, chats and emails.

    Casual

    With colleagues

    "Arvo", "mate", "smoko". Great in conversation; skip them in formal emails, with clients, or with senior leaders you don't know well.

    Avoid at work

    Use plain English

    "Bloody", "sickie" and swearing. In formal emails, HR matters (201 files, compliance) and performance reviews, plain professional English is always better.
